## About

krz builds native apps and self-hosted, privacy-respecting frontends. Two
people, a few servers, no investors. Local-first data, few dependencies, no
ads, no tracking.

Fixed scope: a tool that’s done stays done. Local-first: your data stays on
your device, not our servers. Fewer dependencies: less to break, less to
trust. Maintenance over features: we’d rather fix than pad.

Active. Slow. Not recruiting.
